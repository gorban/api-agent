## Completion display and scheduling

- [x] Limit each sanitized terminal completion line to 1,024 characters, including an explicit truncation marker. Scrub the complete formatted identity before applying the display limit; cutting a registered secret first must never expose fragments. Preserve exact identities for selection/counting and full original result evidence. Verify boundary lengths, huge identities and a registered secret spanning the display cutoff, with independent cap/order mutations.
- [x] Service structured completion events ahead of raw stdout backlogs. While the worker remains active, bound raw-output batches so forwarded controller/plugin output cannot indefinitely delay event processing or cancellation. Preserve complete final captured output and retained inspection handoff. Prove ordering with controlled process/IPC barriers and meaningful scheduling mutations rather than relying on worker print timing.

- [x] Retained-worker transfer must wait for a producer stdout boundary acknowledged by the reader, or clean EOF, before sealing accepted output. Preserve stdout written before completion even when the reader lags; a temporarily empty queue is insufficient. Keep boundary tokens out of output, preserve a preceding non-newline prefix, and distinguish post-completion inspection chatter. Bound the handoff wait with the shared nested timeout/progress interface, retain cancellation and process-failure cleanup, and expose missing/error acknowledgements rather than hanging or claiming complete evidence. Verify slow-reader process proofs, marker/EOF/error cases and an independent omitted-boundary mutation.

- [x] Do not transport stdout-boundary tokens through the environment, including during interpreter startup hooks. Keep each token local to its generated controller bootstrap call, outside ordinary module-global string dumps; subprocesses must not inherit it. Routine diagnostic dumps cannot acknowledge completion early or truncate pre-completion evidence; verify with a real slow-reader environment-dump regression and a targeted environment-exposure mutation. This is protocol correlation, not a security boundary against deliberate process introspection.
