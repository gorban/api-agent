# Provider-owned credential protection

[Shared LLM adapter requirements](implementation_plan_f1.md) · [Evidence propagation](implementation_plan_b1c1a2d.md)

- [ ] Providers must register credentials with their operation-owned authority before exposure or use and protect known credentials in LLM requests before upstream dispatch, and in responses before returning them to callers or users. Authentication stays lazy. This is independent of LOG_CONTENT and TRACELOOP_TRACE_CONTENT; neither flag authorizes credential disclosure.
- [ ] Apply registration to credential-using catalog and model-discovery operations as well as inference. Verify the Bedrock catalog path joins the selected operation authority before its worker uses credentials, without forcing authentication during help, configuration or collection.
- [ ] Apply protection through all admitted completion and streaming paths, including provider-added wire content, tools, structured outputs and retries. Preserve legitimate typed values and local validation; fail explicitly when safe removal cannot preserve the contract. Do not silently change models, credentials, accounting or inference behavior unrelated to protection.
- [ ] Scrub permitted evidence before IPC, durable storage or SDK queues using the same credential-owning authority. Define and enforce registration/emission synchronization and an explicit custom-provider capability or compatible transition; unproven authority must not emit credential-bearing content. Document that unknown or later-discovered secrets cannot be reliably identified retroactively.
- [ ] Verify source-to-provider and provider-to-caller protection, actual credential registration, split/escaped carriers, concurrent and late registration, structured/tool and streaming cases, both content flags, failure and cancellation, and no secret in IPC, reports or logs. Use causal controls, exact applicable coverage, independent/external review and human acceptance.
- [ ] When credential admission defers provider construction, keep startup single-owned across concurrent callers. Cancelling one waiter must not cancel another caller's startup. Closing must prevent late owner creation or readiness, drain pending startup before disposal, and retain failed cleanup for retry. Preserve bounded existing cleanup and retry semantics without adding inference retries. Verify concurrent starts, close during admission, cancellation, failed-start retry and resource ownership.
- [ ] Record human acceptance.
