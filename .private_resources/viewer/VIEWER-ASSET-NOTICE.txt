The workflow viewer uses the public Marked 18.1.1 and DOMPurify 3.4.17
bundles already distributed with api-agent. Their license texts are retained
as marked-LICENSE.txt and dompurify-LICENSE.txt.

For direct file:// opening, the viewer copies those browser bundles and
changes only their final ECMAScript module export to assign the exported
object to globalThis.marked or globalThis.DOMPurify. Source-map references
are removed. Their original copyright/license headers are retained.

PlantUML is an optional development renderer and is not bundled in the
application wheel. PlantUML-NOTICE.txt records the pinned MIT JAR's own
`-license` output and its bundled component acknowledgements.
